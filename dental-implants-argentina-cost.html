Dental implants in Argentina: cost

Updated 27 Oct 2026. Few independent surveys exist; the figures below are published by clinics themselves.

  • Buenos Aires, complete implant with crown: USD 1,000–1,800; All-on-4/6 USD 8,000–12,000 per arch; grafts/sinus lift +USD 300–1,500; 3D CT about USD 100 (Dra. Blois).
  • Buenos Aires, implant only: USD 800–1,000 (AmoSmile).
  • Buenos Aires, all-in incl. extraction/graft and crown: from USD 3,000–3,500; full-mouth USD 24,000–30,000 (AM Estética Dental).
  • Buenos Aires, implants ARS 480,000–1,200,000 depending on brand (Solución Dental).
  • Córdoba: from ARS 500,000 (Sublimis). No USD figure found.

The huge spread (USD 1,000 vs 3,000+) is mostly about what is included. Argentine prices change frequently with inflation and imported materials, as one clinic notes.
