Dental implants in Argentina

Argentina is a less common destination for US patients, but prices published by Buenos Aires clinics are in the same range as Mexico. Many clinics quote in USD but charge in pesos.

What to know

  • Number of trips: conventional implants usually need healing time between placing the implant and fitting the final crown, so plan for two trips; clinics state this differently, so ask for the timeline in writing.
  • Aftercare: arrange who will see you at home if something goes wrong before you go. The CDC advises planning follow-up care and getting copies of records.
  • Credentials: ask for the dentist's licence number and check it with the official registry.
  • Argentina checks: dentists hold a matrícula (national "M.N." or provincial "M.P."); check it on the national health professionals registry (REFEPS, run by the Ministry of Health).
  • Payment: USD prices are often paid in pesos at a stated rate; confirm which rate.
