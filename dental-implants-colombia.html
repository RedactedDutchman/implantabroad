Dental implants in Colombia

Colombia (mainly Medellín, Bogotá and Cartagena) is a growing fly-in destination for US patients. Many clinics publish USD prices; some figures below come from a facilitator (agency) that books clinics, which is labelled.

What to know

  • Number of trips: conventional implants usually need healing time between placing the implant and fitting the final crown, so plan for two trips; clinics state this differently, so ask for the timeline in writing.
  • Aftercare: arrange who will see you at home if something goes wrong before you go. The CDC advises planning follow-up care and getting copies of records.
  • Credentials: ask for the dentist's licence number and check it with the official registry.
  • Colombia checks: dentists must be registered in the national health workforce registry (ReTHUS); ask for the dentist's registration and check it.
  • Travel: check current US State Department advisories for the city.
