What to know

  • Number of trips: conventional implants usually need healing time between placing the implant and fitting the final crown, so plan for two trips; clinics state this differently, so ask for the timeline in writing.
  • Aftercare: arrange who will see you at home if something goes wrong before you go. The CDC advises planning follow-up care and getting copies of records.
  • Credentials: ask for the dentist's licence number and check it with the official registry.
  • Costa Rica checks: dentists are registered with the Colegio de Cirujanos Dentistas de Costa Rica; ask for the registration number.
