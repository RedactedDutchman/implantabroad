Is dental tourism safe?

There's no yes/no answer and we can't give one. Here's what official bodies say:

  • The CDC lists risks of medical tourism including infections (including antibiotic-resistant ones), differing provider standards, and complications on flying after procedures, and advises seeing a provider at home first and planning follow-up care. See also the CDC Yellow Book.
  • The ADA's consumer site MouthHealthy has travel guidance, and the ADA ethics council has published a statement on dental tourism.
  • The UK General Dental Council has a guide, Going abroad for your dental care.

Checks before you book

  • Verify the dentist's licence in the official registry (see methodology).
  • Get a written, itemised treatment plan and timeline.
  • Ask what happens, and who pays, if something fails.
  • Arrange a dentist at home for follow-up.
  • Bring copies of X-rays/CT scans and records home.

This page summarises public sources. It is not medical advice and does not guarantee any outcome.
