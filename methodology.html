Methodology & disclaimer

Not medical advice

ImplantAbroad provides price research only. It is not medical or dental advice and makes no guarantees about outcomes, safety or prices. Talk to a licensed dentist about your case.

Check your dentist's registration

  • Mexico: cédula profesional (SEP registry), clinic COFEPRIS notice.
  • Costa Rica: Colegio de Cirujanos Dentistas de Costa Rica.
  • Argentina: matrícula nacional/provincial (REFEPS).

How price data is collected

We only use figures published by a named source (cost surveys, price guides, clinic price pages). Each figure in prices.json has a source URL and an "as of" date. Ranges shown are the lowest low and highest high across sources, which mix different inclusions. If we can't find a figure, we leave it out and say so. We do not invent or average-in our own numbers.

How clinics are listed

Clinics in clinics.json were found via web search and their websites checked to exist. All are marked "unverified listing – not yet partnered". Listing is not endorsement.

Affiliate / lead disclosure

We may in future earn money from clinics through paid featured or verified listings and per-lead fees, and possibly affiliate links. Paid placements will be labelled. Payments never change published price figures. Currently no clinic pays us.
